What is the difference between NVR and DVR for CCTV in Dammam?

A DVR (Digital Video Recorder) works with analog cameras over coaxial cable and is a reliable, cost-effective solution for standard security setups. An NVR (Network Video Recorder) works with IP cameras over a network connection and offers significantly higher resolution, better image quality especially at night, and greater flexibility for larger or more complex properties. QIA installs and configures both NVR and DVR systems across Dammam and will recommend the right option based on your property size, camera count, and budget.
