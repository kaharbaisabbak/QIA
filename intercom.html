Audio Intercom vs Video Intercom — Which Is Right for You?

Audio Intercom — The Budget Option

Audio-only intercom lets you speak with visitors at the door without seeing them. It is the most cost-effective entry control solution and a reliable choice for apartments and homes with simpler security needs. QIA installs audio intercom systems quickly and at an affordable price across Dammam.

Video Intercom — The Premium Option

Video intercom adds a camera to the outdoor unit so you can see exactly who is at the door from an indoor screen or your mobile phone before deciding whether to open it. This is the preferred choice for villas and apartment buildings across Dammam looking for stronger entrance security and full remote access control.

Wired Intercom — Stable & Reliable

Wired intercom systems offer the most stable connection and the best video quality, making them ideal for villas, new construction, and larger buildings where cables can be run cleanly during the build or renovation phase. Wireless intercom systems install without the need to run new cables through walls or ceilings — ideal for ready-made homes and apartments in Dammam where retrofitting wired cabling would be disruptive. Quick to install while still offering full audio, video, and remote unlock functionality.
